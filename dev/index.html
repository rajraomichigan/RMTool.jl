RMTool

A Random Matrix and Free Probability Calculator for Julia

Users Guide — Version 1.0 (Julia port of the MATLAB toolbox by N. Raj Rao, Department of EECS, Massachusetts Institute of Technology, February 1, 2006; original at http://www.mit.edu/~raj/rmtool).

RMTool is a free toolbox for computing the limiting spectral measure of a large class of random matrices. The techniques behind it are based on the polynomial method (see N. Raj Rao and Alan Edelman, The polynomial method for random matrices, arXiv: math.PR/0601389). Some applications are mentioned in the afore-mentioned paper.
